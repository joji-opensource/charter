**As a** customer
**I want to** type what I am looking for and see matching products
**With** results that tolerate small spelling mistakes, and the price and stock shown on each result
**Must also** show results within one second · work on a phone
**So that** I find what I came for without browsing every category

## Done means

- **a.** Searching for a product's name shows that product first
- **b.** A search with one wrong letter ("runing shoes") still finds the product
- **c.** Every result shows its price and whether it is in stock
- **d.** Results appear within one second on a phone
