---
name: status
description: Where the product stands, in plain English: what is proven, at risk, in progress, and what comes next.
allowed-tools: Bash(dev-charter status:*), Bash(dev-charter report:*)
---

Run `dev-charter status`, then `dev-charter report`. Tell the product owner, in plain words and in this
order: anything At risk and why; what is proven or live; what is being built and on which branches; and the next
steps. Point them to LEDGER.xlsx for the detail.
