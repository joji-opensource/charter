title: Delivery dates come from the carrier
date: 2026-10-20

**Decision** The expected delivery date shown to customers is the carrier's own date.
**Instead of** Estimating the date ourselves from past deliveries.
**Because** Customers trust the carrier's date, and an estimate of ours would disagree with the carrier's own messages.
