---
id: DEC-002
title: Saved cards are kept by the payment provider
date: 2026-10-05
decided_by: Maya Patel
features: [F-013]
---

**Decision** Saved cards are kept by the payment provider; we keep only the token it gives us.
**Instead of** Storing card numbers in our own database.
**Because** It keeps card numbers out of our systems entirely, which is safer for customers and far less to certify.
