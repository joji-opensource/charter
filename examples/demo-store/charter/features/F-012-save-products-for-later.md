**As a** customer
**I want to** save products to a wishlist
**With** a save button on each product, and a way to remove a product or add it to my cart from the list
**Must also** show me only my own list · keep it when I sign in on another device
**So that** I can come back and buy them later without searching again

## Done means

- **a.** A signed-in customer can save a product from its page, and it appears in their wishlist
- **b.** A product can be removed from the wishlist
- **c.** A product can be added to the cart from the wishlist
- **d.** The wishlist is the same after signing in on a different device
