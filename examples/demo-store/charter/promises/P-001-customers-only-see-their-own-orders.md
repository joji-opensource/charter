**We promise** you only ever see your own orders, addresses and payment details, never anyone else's
**Applies to** every page, email and text message that shows an order or an account

## Done means

- **a.** Opening another customer's order or account page shows a sign-in page
- **b.** Order emails and text messages go only to the order's own customer
