**We promise** the price you see before you pay is exactly what you are charged
**Applies to** product pages, the cart and the checkout

## Done means

- **a.** The amount charged equals the total on the confirm screen, to the cent
- **b.** If a price changes while a product is in the cart, the customer is told before paying
