**As a** customer
**I want to** reset my password from the sign-in page
**With** an email link that works once and expires after an hour
**Must also** never say whether an email address has an account
**So that** I can get back into my account without calling support

## Done means

- **a.** The reset email arrives within a minute
- **b.** A used or expired link explains what to do next
- **c.** The sign-in page shows the same message whether or not the email address has an account
